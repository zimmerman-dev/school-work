Lab Steps

Get with your pairing partner and complete the following steps.

  1. Read the specifications for Part 2 of the Project. Part of the specs are reproduced here:
    • The output to the screen should display the employee’s number and name, gross pay, withholding amount, and net pay. At the end of the report, display the total gross payroll amount, the total net payroll amount, and the average net pay paid per employee.
    • Each employee is to be paid time-and-a-half for all hours worked over 35.
    • A tax amount of 15% of gross salary is to be withheld.
  2. Discuss with your partner what the author intends the above specifications to mean.
  3. Based on your understanding of the specifications, work together as a group to prepare some test data for the first 5 records of the Employee Payroll Record set.
