Homemade Scratch Pasta

Directions

Homemade pasta is one those recipes many people assume is complicated if they'be never tried it before. With this recipe, we'll show that it's actually easy and scalable to make your own using ingredients most of you already have in your pantry.

Ingredients

The only ingredients you will need are:

  • Flour: Two cups of all-purpose flour
  • Eggs: Three large, room-temperature eggs
  • Olive Oil: Two tablespoons of olive oil
  • Salt: One teaspoon of salt
  • Water: Two tablespoons of water

Directions

  1. Mix flour, eggs, olive oil, and salt in a bowl until combined. Add water, 1 teaspoon at a time, until the dough becomes thick and smooth.
  2. Turn dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and knead for 10 minutes. Let dough rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. If you have a pasta machine, divide dough into 8 balls and use pasta machine to roll and cut dough into desired pasta shape.
  4. If you don't have a pasta machine, still divide into 8 dough balls, but use a roller to roll your dough balls into flat almost translucent sheets.
  5. Take one of your sheets and sprinkle a pinch of flour over the top of the sheet. Ater, lightly roll sheet into a roll.
  6. Take your roll, cut the imperfect ends off both sides. Proceed to cut your dough into strips. Width of the strips is at your discretion, but ⅛ of an inch is a standard spaghetti noodle.
  7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 until finished.
  8. Bring water to boil in a large saucepan. Fresh pasta should take no more than 4 to 6 minutes depending on how well done you like your noodles.
