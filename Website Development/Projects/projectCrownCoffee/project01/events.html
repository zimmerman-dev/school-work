Open Mic Fridays

Crown Coffee hosts Open Mic every Friday from 7:00pm to 9:00pm. Guests can sign up for a 30-minute performance slot, and a PA system is provided. Whether you’re performing or just stopping in for coffee, everyone is welcome.

  • 30-minute performance slots
  • Sign-up begins at 12:00pm the day of
  • PA system provided

Performances must adhere to being family friendly.

Holiday & Special Events

Crown Coffee offers seasonal events throughout the year and may adjust hours during holidays. Check this page for updates on special events and any changes to regular business hours.

  • December 24 - Closing early at 4:00pm
  • December 25 - Closed Christmas Day
  • December 31 - New Years Party until 10:00pm
  • January 1 - Closed New Years Day

Private Event Rentals

Crown Coffee is available for private rentals after regular business hours. The café can host small gatherings and offers basic catering options upon request. Contact us for more information about availability.

  • Available after 7:00pm
  • Seats up to 40 guests
  • Catering packages available
