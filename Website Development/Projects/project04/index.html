Welcome to The Student Study Collective

A relaxed campus study group for students who want support, structure, and a better place to focus.

Study Better Together

Student Study Collective is a hybrid study community for students who want structure, accountability, and a supportive place to focus, whether they are meeting on campus or joining online through Discord.

Virtual Study Rooms

Our Discord server gives members a place to join quiet study rooms, ask quick questions, share resources, and check in with other students throughout the week.

Campus Meetups

We also host casual in-person study sessions for students who want a focused environment, group accountability, or a place to work through assignments with classmates.
