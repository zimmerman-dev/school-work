#+title: Review Word Problems

* Review Questions

**  🏡 Remix 1: Real Estate Agents (Ratio Word Problem)

Trina and Devin both work for Northbrook Properties. Over the past quarter, they closed a total of 204 home sales.
Trina sold 4 times as many homes as Devin.
How many homes did each of them sell?

-----

** 🖊️ Remix 2: Book Sales (System with Cost & Quantity)

Taylor and Jordan sold textbooks for a campus fundraiser. Taylor managed to sell six times more books than Jordan.
Together, they sold 133 books in total.
How many books did each person sell?

-----

** 🎟️ Remix 3: Fundraiser (Extreme Sales Ratio with Total)

Liam and Avery were selling raffle tickets for their school’s music program. Tickets were $10 each. Avery got a shoutout during morning announcements for outselling everyone — she sold 14 times as many tickets as Liam.
Together, they sold 225 tickets.
How many did each of them sell?

-----

** 🍦 Remix 4: Ice Cream Shop (Cost + Proportional Quantity)

A dessert truck sells cookies for $3 and milkshakes for $7. Last month, they sold 4 times more cookies than shakes, and made $5,160 in total sales.
How many cookies and shakes did they sell?
